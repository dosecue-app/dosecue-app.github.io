Key Capabilities

Designed for Simplicity & Peace of Mind

DoseCue equips you with everything you need to stay on top of your regimen without unnecessary complexity.

⏰

Smart Dose Schedules

Set exact times, recurring daily routines, or flexible intervals. Receive timely notifications directly on your iPhone with quick actions to Mark Taken, Skip, or Snooze.

📦

Inventory & Refill Alerts

Track remaining pills, capsules, or liquid doses. Get low-stock warnings before you run out, plus expiration reminders to ensure your medications remain safe.

📊

History & Progress Reports

Review your adherence history across Week, Month, or Year filters. Access comprehensive Report charts and export clean PDF/CSV summaries for your doctor visits.

🛡️

100% Local-First Privacy

Your sensitive health data stays strictly on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. No mandatory registration, no accounts, and no data selling.

📱

Widgets & Customization

Glance at today's remaining doses right from your Home Screen with WidgetKit. Supports Dark Mode, custom app icons, and multiple localized languages.

👑

Premium Lifetime Access

Unlock unlimited medication schedules, advanced trend reports, and export options with a single one-time purchase. No subscription traps required.

⚠️
Medical Safety Notice
DoseCue is intended for personal organization, reminder scheduling, and adherence tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, recommend dosages, diagnose conditions, or replace the professional judgment of your doctor, pharmacist, or healthcare provider.
