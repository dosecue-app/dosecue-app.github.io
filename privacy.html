Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 22, 2026 • Published by DoseCue Team

This Privacy Policy explains how DoseCue ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our iOS application.

🛡️ Local-First Privacy Guarantee: DoseCue stores your medication schedule and dose records locally on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not sell your health data.

1. Information Stored in the App

Depending on the features you use, DoseCue may store:

  • Medication names, dosage, instructions, and schedules.
  • Reminder preferences and notification identifiers.
  • Taken, skipped, missed, and snoozed dose records.
  • Medication inventory, refill thresholds, and expiration dates.
  • Adherence reports and statistics.
  • Profile preferences and App settings.

In the current local-first version, this information is stored primarily on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and is not uploaded to our own server.

No account is required to use the App.

2. Notifications

DoseCue requests notification permission to deliver medication, refill, inventory, and expiration reminders that you configure.

Notifications are scheduled and managed through Apple’s notification services directly on your device. You can change notification access at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

The current version of DoseCue does not integrate third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and does not send app-usage analytics to our servers.

DoseCue does not send medication names, dosages, instructions, dose notes, inventory quantities, dose history, or exported file contents to analytics services.

Apple may provide aggregated App Store metrics or diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy terms. These reports are handled by Apple and do not give us access to your medication records.

4. Purchases

Premium purchases are processed securely by Apple through StoreKit 2.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, subscription status, and transaction information required to unlock Premium features and restore purchases.

5. Exported Data

When you create a CSV or PDF export, the file is generated locally on your device.

You choose where the file is sent through the iOS Share Sheet. Once you save or share it with Files, Mail, AirDrop, cloud storage, or another service, that destination’s privacy practices apply.

We do not automatically upload or intercept exported files.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

  • We do not sell your personal or medication information.
  • We do not use medication information for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Apple processes Premium purchases. GitHub Pages hosts this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service. Their processing is subject to their own privacy policies. DoseCue does not send your medication records to these services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local medication information remains on your device until you delete it, reset App data, or uninstall the App.

The Delete Data feature in the Settings tab allows you to permanently remove selected categories such as medications, schedules, dose history, reports, inventory, and settings.

Deleting App data does not remove:

  • App Store purchase history.
  • Active Apple subscriptions (managed via Apple ID).
  • Files previously exported and saved outside the App.
  • Information independently held by third-party services.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to reduce unauthorized access to App data. However, no device, storage system, or electronic transmission method is completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining device security, screen-lock protection, backups, and access to exported files.

9. Children’s Privacy

DoseCue is not specifically directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly request personal contact information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise use of the App by a minor, especially when medication schedules are involved.

10. International Users

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information under laws such as GDPR or CCPA.

Because core medication data is stored locally, many of these controls are available directly through the App’s export and deletion features in Settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
